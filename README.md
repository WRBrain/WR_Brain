## 🚀 Key Features

- **🤖 Master Robots Encyclopedia:** Complete coverage of all meta S-Tier bots (Condor, Raptor, Pathfinder, Curie, Shenlou, Dagon, Ochokochi, etc.), staple brawlers, and all Ultimate Editions.
- **👑 Complete Titans & Titan Weapons:** All Titans (Bersagliere, Bedwyr, Eiffel, Newton, Luchador, Indra, etc.) and full Alpha / Beta weapon families.
- **🔫 123 Weapons & 29 Sibling Families:** Heavy ↔ Medium ↔ Light weapon matrices with 1-click sibling variant comparison and live stats per level.
- **🧑‍✈️ 27 Legendary Pilots & 13 Master Skills:** Authentic pilot abilities (Apex Predator, Comet Vanguard, Turret Overclock, etc.) with customizable 7-slot skill matrices.
- **🛸 Drones & Motherships:** Tactical drones (Pascal, Shai, Hiruko, Kestrel, Daltokki, etc.) and orbital strikes (Avalon, Paladin, Keel, Roulette, etc.).
- **🛡️ WRBrain Hangar Synergy Audit:** Real-time range harmony, tactical role spread, and counter-matrix auditing (*Anti-Heal, Shieldbreaker, Quantum Radar, Freeze, Grey Damage*).
- **💥 Live DPS & Battle Lab:** Calculate 1-second burst output, sustained cycle DPS, and test Nuclear Amplifier (+0% to +80%) and pilot damage multipliers.
- **🎚️ 60 FPS Draggable Level Sliders:** Smooth in-place MK1 Lv 1–12, MK2 Lv 1–12, MK3, and Titan Lv 1–150 level previews without UI re-renders.
- **💾 LocalStorage & JSON Export/Import:** Browser persistence and instant loadout export.
